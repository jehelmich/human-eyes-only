Noncommercial License 1.0.0. Anything commercial needs separate terms.

**Terms are not yet drafted. Pending legal review.**

## What requires a commercial license

- production deployment by a business
- embedding in a commercial product
- offering HEO as part of a managed or hosted service
- OEM use and commercial redistribution

## What does not

Everything PolyForm Noncommercial already permits: personal use, hobby and
amateur projects, research and experimentation, security research including
published attacks and benchmarks, and use by charitable, educational, public
research, public safety and health, environmental, and government organisations.

Funding source does not change this. A noncommercial organisation remains
noncommercial for these purposes even where its work is commercially funded —
see the Noncommercial Organizations section of the license.

## Not a software tier

White-glove implementation is a separate service offering. The commercial
license covers the software; it does not bundle integration work, and the
commercial build is not a different codebase.
