Operating summary

Group revenue reached $4.2M in the second quarter, up from $3.1M a year earlier. Operating margin improved to 18.4%, and the board approved a further $12.5M of capital expenditure for the 2027 financial year. Working capital absorbed a further amount in the period, largely timing.

The industrial segment did not meet its internal target: order intake fell 7.2% against a plan that assumed 3.5% growth. Management has not revised full-year guidance, which remains at 22% adjusted margin. The refinancing completed in May without drawing on the revolving facility.

A decision on the Halloway acquisition is expected by 14 September 2026. The offer values the business at 2.4x trailing revenue, below the 3.1x the seller sought in Q1 2026. Currency movements were immaterial at the group level this quarter. The audit committee met twice during the reporting period.
