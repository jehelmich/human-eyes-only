# notes

Durable research notes: design rationale that outlived its conversation, reading
summaries, and results worth keeping.

Working material — long-form reasoning, exploratory drafts, conversation
transcripts — does not belong here. Decisions that came out of it belong in
[docs/decisions.md](../../docs/decisions.md); plans belong in
[ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md); anything measurable belongs in `benchmark/`.

Checkpoint reports live in `checkpoints/CP-N.md` and are written by hand.
