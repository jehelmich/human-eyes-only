# research

Working notes, references, and experiments backing HEO's design claims.

- `notes/` — design rationale and open questions.
- `papers/` — reading list and summaries of relevant prior work.
- `experiments/` — throwaway probes. Anything load-bearing graduates into
  `benchmark/` with a reproducible runner.

Nothing here is part of the published packages, and nothing here should be
treated as a project claim until it has a benchmark backing it.
